Sources and methods

Economic series come from FRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis), which republishes data from BEA, BLS, the Census Bureau, FHFA, the Treasury, and the Philadelphia and Chicago Federal Reserve Banks. Bank figures are computed from FDIC BankFind quarterly call report data; credit union figures from NCUA quarterly 5300 call report files. Each chart links to its underlying series.

State coverage has gaps the national data does not. There is no state-level CPI, so Chicago metro and Midwest regional indexes stand in. State GDP and personal income arrive roughly a quarter after national figures. Bank and credit union data are by headquarters state, and institutions are counted where they are chartered, not where they lend.
